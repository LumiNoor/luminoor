Product Quality & Safety

Authenticity Guarantee

Every pair of LumiNoor lenses is sealed, sterile, and original. We do not sell loose, unbranded, refurbished, or street-market lenses.

Shelf Life

All lenses in our inventory have at least a 1-year shelf life from the date of purchase. The expiration date is clearly marked on each box.

Storage & Handling

Lenses are stored in sealed, sterile packaging. Once you open your lenses, follow standard contact lens hygiene: always clean your hands before handling, use fresh solution daily, and replace lenses on schedule.

Medical Disclaimer

Colored contact lenses are medical devices. If you have never worn contact lenses, consult an eye doctor before your first purchase. LumiNoor does not provide medical advice or eye exams. Always follow your optometrist's guidance on lens fit, wear schedule, and care.

Lens Care & Hygiene

Before First Use

  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and dry completely
  • Consult an eye care professional if you're a first-time wearer
  • Inspect the lens for damage before inserting

Daily Wear

  • Clean your hands before touching lenses or your eyes
  • Use only fresh contact lens solution each time you store or rinse your lenses
  • Never reuse solution in your lens case
  • Replace your lens case every 3 months
  • Don't wear lenses while swimming or in the shower

When to Replace

Follow the lens replacement schedule on your package (typically daily, bi-weekly, or monthly). Overwearing lenses increases the risk of eye irritation and infection.

Red Flags — Stop Wearing & Seek Help

  • Persistent redness, pain, or discomfort
  • Excessive tearing or discharge
  • Blurred vision or halos around light
  • Feeling that something is stuck in your eye

Remove lenses immediately and contact an eye care professional if you experience any of these symptoms.
